90 s · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · two characters only. The manager never appears and only sends text to the register screen.

**Trait of the episode.** Female platypuses produce milk; Patrick is male. Platypus fur fluoresces blue-green under UV light (Anich et al. 2021, *Mammalia*, doi:10.1515/mammalia-2020-0027; Australian Museum). The glow appears only where the UV lamp shines and disappears when it is switched off. No claim is made about what the glow is for.
