# 本能服務｜Instinct Service — EP.02《閉眼服務｜Service with Eyes Closed》

90 s · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · two speaking characters only. The manager never appears and only sends text to the register screen.

**Trait of the episode.** Platypuses hunt underwater with their eyes, ears and nostrils closed. They find prey with electroreceptors and mechanoreceptors in the bill (Australian Museum, *Platypus*). Electroreception is a sense. Patrick can detect weak electrical signals but cannot generate, store or pass on electricity, so the phone stays at 1%. The demonstration only locates a live shrimp; it is not a freshness test.

**Source of truth.** The data files in this folder (`script.json`, `props.json`, `shots.json`, `acting.json`, `sound.json`) are authoritative. This page is for reading. The times below are the actual voice placements after TTS.
